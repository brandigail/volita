# Volita: 120 things to post about

How to use this: pick any idea, film your phone screen (or just talk to the camera), paste the caption, post. Every idea uses only things Volita does today.

Rules that keep you out of trouble:
- When you show an example customer, use made-up names (like the videos do). Never show a real customer's message or number.
- Say "Try Pro free for 14 days, no card" and give the link: https://volita.netlify.app
- Outlook/Hotmail email is not connected yet. Say "Gmail, Yahoo, AOL, iCloud and business email".
- Don't promise exact time saved. Say "I stopped typing quotes at night" instead of "saves 10 hours".
- Pro-only features: morning briefing, email, tone rewrite, writing style, web lookups. Basic and up: price list, quote as PDF, photo captions, templates, send-later reminders, other languages.

## Ready-to-film scripts (word for word)

**Script 1: the quote (25 s).** *Camera on you:* "It's 6pm and I'm still typing quotes. Not anymore." *Screen:* open Volita, tap the quote, say the job. *You:* "Quote for Mrs Jones, new geyser, four thousand eight hundred, labour, VAT." *Screen:* the finished quote. *You:* "Done. That took twenty seconds." *End:* "Try it free for 14 days. Link in my bio."

**Script 2: the customer who waited (20 s).** "A customer messages you at two. You're on a roof. You answer at nine at night. They've hired someone else. Now: I say my reply while I'm up the ladder. Volita writes it. I send it from the roof. Link in bio."

**Script 3: ask your day (20 s).** Screen: Assistant tab. You say "What's on today?" Volita answers with the day. "My whole day, read to me, before I've had coffee."

**Script 4: email by voice (30 s).** "Any email from Jan?" "Yes, Jan wants a quote for a deck." "Reply that it's twelve and a half thousand and I can start on the fifth." Show the email waiting. "And nothing sends until I tap Send."

**Script 5: the follow-up (20 s).** "Which quotes has nobody answered?" Show the list. "Three of these became jobs after I chased them."  (Only use this line if it is true for you.)

**Script 6: pick your voice (15 s).** Play the six voices. "Which one is your favourite? Comment it."

**Script 7: Afrikaans (20 s).** Say a sentence in Afrikaans, show the draft. "Praat net, en Volita skryf dit."

**Script 8: the honest review ask (20 s).** "I made an app. I need honest feedback. Download it, try it for five minutes, then tap Profile, scroll to Reviews, and tell me what's wrong. I'll fix it."

**Script 9: safety (20 s).** Say a fake card number. Show the "Volita cannot send this" pop-up. "Volita refuses card numbers, bank details and passwords. On purpose."

**Script 10: the home screen (15 s).** Film the home screen, tap Volita, say a job. "It lives on my home screen like every other app."
